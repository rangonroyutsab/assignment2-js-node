About

Rediscover Sanctuary Cap Cana, the Dominican Republic's most exclusive adults-only all-inclusive resort.

You'll be delighted by the resort's $45 million modernization as you explore spectacular suites, artfully crafted lobbies, updated restaurants and bars, enhanced world-class spa and state-of-the-art fitness center. Sanctuary Cap Cana, A Luxury Collection Adult All-Inclusive Resort is located in Cap Cana, a private gated community retreat inside Punta Cana. Cap Cana is made up of 30,000 acres of flawless beaches...

The resort offers a refined adults-only escape with spacious suites, ocean views, elevated dining, premium bars, relaxing pools, and peaceful areas designed for privacy and comfort. Guests can enjoy a complete all-inclusive experience with access to restaurants, entertainment, wellness facilities, beach areas, and personalized service throughout their stay.

Whether you are planning a romantic getaway, a luxury beach vacation, or a relaxing all-inclusive stay, Sanctuary Cap Cana provides a calm and elegant setting close to Punta Cana's airport, marina, golf, and coastline.
